What it does

Four features, one smart system

Instead of solving one problem, SmartStudy Monitor brings together attention, comprehension, and analytics in a single lightweight app.

🕐

Session Tracking

Automatically records when you start and stop studying. No manual input needed — just open and go.

👁

Focus Detection

Uses your webcam and OpenCV to detect face presence. If you leave or look away, it logs a distraction event instantly.

🧠

Confusion Predictor

Paste your notes and the ML model tells you if the topic is easy or potentially confusing — so you know where to focus.

📊

Session Analytics

All session data is saved locally. View total sessions, average duration, and track your improvement over time.

How it works

Simple workflow, smart results

The entire system runs in your terminal with a simple menu. No internet required.

1

Start your study session

The timer starts the moment you choose "Start Study". The app begins recording your session duration in the background.

2

Monitor your focus via webcam

OpenCV captures a frame and checks for your face using a Haar Cascade classifier. Face present = focused. Face missing = distraction logged.

3

Analyze your notes with ML

Paste your study notes into the app. A Logistic Regression model converts the text into features and predicts whether the topic is easy or risky.

4

Review your analytics

End the session and your data is saved to a local CSV file. View total sessions and average study duration anytime.
